Support

Keep the lights
on around here.

Aether is a small studio: websites and Discord bots built carefully, one project at a time. Donations are never required — but if something here saved you time, you can put something back into it.

Donate ↗

Where it goes

Domain and DNS, Cloudflare, the payment provider's fees, and the time spent on maintenance, security reviews and reply-within-3-days support.

What it does not change

Nothing here is pay-to-win. Donations do not buy priority over paid orders, and they never unlock anything a customer would otherwise pay for.

No account needed

Donating is optional and separate from your Aether account. You do not need to sign in, and no order is created for a donation.

Donation destination not configured yet.
This page is wired to a single configuration value — no payment credentials or private addresses are ever placed in frontend code. To switch donations on, the site owner sets donateUrl in public/aether-config.js to a donation page they own (for example a Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors, or a donation link created in the payment dashboard). Until then this button stays inert instead of pointing at an invented address.
