Privacy
Policy.

This page describes, in plain language, exactly what this website stores about you, why it is needed, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. It describes the system as it is actually built — not a template.

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Short version: an account needs your email address and a password hash. Orders need what you ordered, the amount, the payment status and the provider's identifiers. The support chat needs the messages you write. We run no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts, and we do not sell data to anyone. Payments are handled by NOWPayments — we never see your wallet keys, card data or bank details.

1. Controller and contact

The controller responsible for the processing described here ("we") is the operator of Aether, whose legal identity and postal address are stated in the Imprint. For every privacy question, request or complaint, write to questions@get-aether.de. No data protection officer is appointed — we are not required to appoint one, and this page says so rather than inventing a name.

What we do not process

  • No analytics and no advertising. There is no Google Analytics, no pixel, no session recorder and no third-party tracker on this site.
  • No wallet keys and no card data. Crypto payments happen on the provider's own page; we never receive your private keys, seed phrase, card number or bank details.
  • No sensitive categories. We do not ask for health, political, religious or biometric data, and you should not send it to us in the chat.
  • No automated decision-making that produces legal effects about you. Account restrictions are always a human decision recorded in the audit log.

3. Cookies and local storage

This site sets one cookie — the session cookie — and nothing else. It also uses a small amount of browser storage to remember your chat panel and the site's cookie choice. None of it is used for tracking, profiling or advertising. Full detail, including durations and the categories, is in the Cookie Policy.

5. International transfers

This site runs on infrastructure that is global by design: Cloudflare and GitHub operate worldwide networks, and a request can be served from a data centre outside the European Economic Area. Those providers maintain their own transfer safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent instruments) for that traffic. Our database is provisioned in the European region. Where a transfer is necessary, it is covered by the provider's own safeguards rather than by a claim on this page that we cannot verify.

6. How your data is protected

  • Everything travels over HTTPS/TLS; plain HTTP is redirected and the browser is told to upgrade.
  • Passwords are stored only as PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes with a random salt per password, 100,000 iterations. They are never stored reversibly, never logged, and cannot be read by us or by anyone who obtains the database.
  • Sessions are stored in the database as a SHA-256 hash of the token, so a database leak cannot be replayed as a login. The cookie itself is HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax and named __Host-, which pins it to this exact host and path.
  • Every page is served with a strict Content Security Policy, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: DENY, a referrer policy and a permissions policy.
  • Access to another account's orders, conversations or payment details is refused server-side on every request. A Purchase ID or payment ID is an identifier, never a credential.
  • Payment status is only ever accepted from the provider's cryptographically signed callback, never from a browser.
  • Sensitive actions (role changes, restrictions, network blocks) are recorded in an audit log with the acting staff account.

No system can be perfectly secure. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please write to questions@get-aether.de and give us a reasonable chance to fix it before you disclose it publicly.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while the account exists. If you delete your account, the account row and its sessions and tokens are deleted immediately.
  • Orders: kept after account deletion, detached from the deleted account, because invoices and payment records have to be retained for tax and bookkeeping purposes (typically up to 10 years in Germany) and because they may be needed to resolve a dispute.
  • Support chat: kept while it is needed to serve the related order or account; messages are detached from a deleted account in the same way as orders.
  • Security data: rate-limit counters are short-lived (minutes to an hour). The IP recorded on an account is overwritten on the next sign-in and is removed with the account. Blocked-network entries stay until an administrator removes them.
  • Audit log: retained, because its purpose is accountability for privileged actions.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you (Art. 15) — much of it is visible directly on your account page;
  • Correct inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • Delete your data (Art. 17) — you can delete your account yourself at any time, and you can write to us about anything that remains;
  • Restrict processing (Art. 18);
  • Data portability (Art. 20) — ask us and we will provide the account and order data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing was based on consent — this does not affect what was lawful before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these, write to questions@get-aether.de from the address on your account (or tell us enough to identify you). We answer within one month. If you think we handled your data wrongly, you have the right to complain to a supervisory authority (Art. 77) — normally the authority of your habitual residence, your place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.

9. Do you have to give us this data?

You are not obliged to give us anything. But without an email address and a password we cannot create an account, and without an account there is no way to buy on this site or to receive an order. The security data described above is generated automatically by any web server; if you do not want it processed, the only option is not to use the site.

10. Children

This service is for adults and is not directed at children. Creating an account requires you to be at least 16 years old. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the way the service works changes. The version at the top of the page always applies, and a material change is announced on the site before it takes effect.
