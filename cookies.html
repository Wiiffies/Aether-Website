Policy.

This site uses one cookie and a few small pieces of browser storage. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking — so there is nothing to switch off, and nothing here is waiting for your consent before it runs.

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Why there is no "Accept all / Reject all" wall: consent is only required for storage that is not strictly necessary — analytics, advertising, social plugins and similar. This site has none of those. Everything listed below is either strictly necessary to deliver the service you asked for (§ 25(2) TDDDG, Art. 5(3) ePrivacy) or is a preference you set yourself. We still show a small notice on your first visit, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the button at the bottom of this page.

1. Strictly necessary

These are required for the site to work. They cannot be switched off while you use the service — without the session cookie you could not sign in, and there is no account-free way to buy.

2. Preferences

These remember choices you make. They contain no identifiers and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Analytics and marketing

None. This site runs no analytics service, no advertising network, no social media pixel, no A/B testing tool and no session recorder. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no TikTok pixel and no heatmap. Nothing about your browsing is profiled, and no data is sold or shared for advertising.

Because there is no non-essential storage, choosing "Reject" in the notice changes nothing about how the site behaves — it only records that you did not want to be asked again. That is a deliberate design choice: we would rather not collect the data than build a consent theatre around it.

4. Change or withdraw your choice

You can reopen the notice and change your answer at any time. This clears any preference stored by that choice; strictly necessary storage stays, because the site cannot work without it.

5. How to remove everything from your browser

Your browser's own settings let you see and delete cookies and site data at any time — usually under "Privacy", "Cookies and site data" or "Clear browsing data". Deleting the session cookie signs you out; nothing else on the site depends on it.
