How it works

  • An account is required to buy — there is no guest checkout. Sign in (or create an account) and your account email and Discord are used automatically, plus your order, invoice and chat stay in one place.
  • Click Pay with Crypto — get a NOWPayments payment (BTC/LTC/ETH), send the exact amount, and the page checks it for you until it confirms
  • Source code is delivered after completion — you host it yourself
  • Something bigger — 15+ pages, a dashboard, sharding, shops, 3D? Set Project Size in the builder or send an inquiry: large projects are quoted individually and you pay only once you approve the quote
  • Hosting, domains, backends and databases are not included
  • Custom quotes use the builder estimate — final price confirmed before work starts
  • Prefer email? Use the detail pages — every tier has an Email instead link
Need hosting?

Static sites: Netlify or GitHub Pages. Sites with logins/backends: Cloudflare or managed hosting. Domains: Prepaid Hoster, Cloudflare or Hostinger — we point you to the right option.
