Locked out? We email a single-use reset link to the address on the account. It expires after 30 minutes and is only ever sent to that inbox — the form never says whether an address has an account, so it cannot be used to look up who signed up.

New here? Create an account — it is the only way to buy. If you ever ordered from Aether before accounts existed, registering with that same email claims those orders automatically. We only store your email, a password hash, your optional Discord name, your orders and this chat.
